AYAKUCHO

Trust, betrayal, and Peruvian folklore
6–12 players30–45 minSocial deductionAges 16+

Each player secretly belongs to one of two teams — the Natives, who inhabit the world we know, or the Supays, spirits of the Ukhu Pacha, the realm below. Discuss, deduce, lie and accuse, and do everything in your power to eliminate your foes.

How to play

  1. Setup. Each player draws a secret role, a voting wheel, and a number token at random, placing the token on the board so everyone can see where they're sitting. Set the stressful-event cards beside the board.
  2. Night phase. Everyone closes their eyes. The Supays — all except the Muki — open their eyes and identify each other for 5 seconds, then close them again.
  3. Initial intrigue. Player 1 secretly looks at Player 2's role. In games of 8 or more, Player 2 also looks at Player 3's role.
Repeat until a team wins

4 · Discussion — 1 minute

Talk and strategize. You may lie, tell the truth, or stay silent — but never show your role card.

5 · Voting

Everyone votes anonymously by setting a number on their voting wheel. Place the wheels face-down together, shuffle them, and count the votes on each player:

  • 1 voteNothing happens.
  • 2 votesThat player secretly looks at the role of any one player they choose.
  • 3 votesThat player dies.
  • The X on a voting wheel counts as a blank vote.
  • 3 votes on a player in a round kills them — and so do 6 votes on a single player in one round.

6 · Stressful event

Draw one stressful-event card and read it aloud.

Death. When you die, flip your number token and reveal your role to everyone. You still join the discussion and keep voting — but every dead player's vote is shuffled together, and only one of them is added to the voting pile. Your number no longer exists, so it is ignored for stressful-event cards.

Advanced mode

The advanced game adds a third team: Sacha & Amaru — two who deserted their sides to form their own. Traitors, rebels, dissenters, heroes… they've been called many things.

What changes

  • Supays no longer know each other.
  • The Muki is just one more Supay.
  • Sacha and Amaru know each other.

New winning conditions

  • Natives win by killing Sacha, Amaru, and 2 other Supays.
  • Supays win by killing Sacha, Amaru, and 2 other Natives.
  • Sacha & Amaru win if … (condition to confirm)
